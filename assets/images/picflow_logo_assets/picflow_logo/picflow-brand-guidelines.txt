PIC FLOW — BRAND GUIDELINES
============================

LOGO
----
Wordmark: "Pic" (bold, dark/white) + "Flow" (bold, RGB tri-color)
Subtext: PHOTOGRAPHY (optional, tracking +3.5, muted gray)
Icon: Three vertical bars — histogram / RGB prism

COLORS
------
Primary RGB (Light mode):
  Red   #D62B2B
  Green #16A34A
  Blue  #2563EB

Primary RGB (Dark mode):
  Red   #F05555
  Green #34D672
  Blue  #60A5FA

Text:
  Dark background text  #111318
  Light background text #F5F5F5
  Subtext               #999999

TYPOGRAPHY
----------
Font: Inter (fallback: system-ui, sans-serif)
Wordmark weight: 800 (Extra Bold)
Subtext weight: 400, letter-spacing 3.5px, ALL CAPS

ICON SIZES
----------
64px  — App icon, large UI
48px  — App icon, medium
32px  — Toolbar, navigation
20px  — Favicon, small UI

USAGE RULES
-----------
- Always keep 3 colors as brand identity — do not remove or swap
- "Flow" letter colors: F=Red, l=Green, ow=Blue
- Minimum clear space around logo: 1× icon height on all sides
- Do not stretch, rotate, or recolor the bars
- Subtext "PHOTOGRAPHY" is optional for compact usage
